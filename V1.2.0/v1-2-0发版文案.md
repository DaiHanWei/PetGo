# TailTopia V1.2.0 发版文案

内容运营与增长版本 + **Toko 商城首发**（2026-08-27 产品拍板：电商线随本版发版）。以下每版均含 **印尼语（上架用）/ English / 中文** 三语对照，三版语义对齐，措辞按各自语言的口语习惯写，不是逐字直译。

**文案范围口径：** 写 `dev/dev_1.1.6` 代码中已实现、对用户可感知的功能：1.2.0 PRD 三份 + 电商线（PRD 在代码仓 `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-petgo-platform-2026-08-19/PRD.md`）。Toko 是本版最大的可见变化，放主标题与第一条。

## A. App Store「What's New」主推版

### A-1 · Bahasa Indonesia（上架用）

Toko resmi buka, Feed juga tampil baru 🛍️🐾

🛍️ **Toko TailTopia hadir di tab baru** — makanan, camilan, obat & vitamin, sampai perawatan buat anabul, semua pilihan dan dikurasi. Tambah ke keranjang, checkout, bayar pakai QRIS atau campur sama PawCoin, kiriman reguler 2–4 hari. Ada juga pengingat pas stok makanan mau habis, plus rekomendasi yang disesuaikan sama umur & berat pet kamu — bukan iklan.

ℹ️ Tab Kesehatan pindah: konsultasi dokter hewan sekarang ada di halaman Catatan Kesehatan (tombol "Mulai konsultasi").

🆕 **Feed tampil beda** — foto full lebar, satu kolom, lebih lega buat dilihat. Suka postingannya? Langsung ❤️ dari beranda aja, nggak perlu buka dulu.

✨ **Rekomendasi yang lebih ngerti kamu** — tab Semua sekarang diracik biar tips, momen lucu, sama postingan kucing & anjing kebagian pas. Scroll nggak bikin bosen.

📸 **Kartu postingan buat di-share** — dari postingan mana pun, bikin kartu gambar cantik lengkap sama QR-nya, langsung share ke Story.

🐾 **Halaman profil anabul tampilan baru** — link profil pet kamu sekarang gaya scrapbook, lebih cakep buat dikirim ke temen. Yang buka cuma bisa lihat timeline, data kesehatan tetap privat.

🪪 **Kartu identitas pet bisa dibagikan gratis** — versi berwatermark, tinggal tap Bagikan. Dapat PawCoin tiap kali share 😉

✂️ **Upload foto makin rapi** — foto yang kepanjangan atau kelebaran bisa langsung di-crop sebelum posting.

🏷️ Postingan pilihan & pengguna keren sekarang dapet label khusus dari tim TailTopia. Pencapaian kecil anabul juga masuk ke Notifikasi.

Yuk update, belanja buat anabul, terus pamer hari ini juga!

### A-2 · English

The Toko is open — and the feed got a makeover 🛍️🐾

🛍️ **Meet the TailTopia Toko, in its own tab** — food, treats, meds & vitamins, and grooming essentials, all hand-picked for your pets. Add to cart, check out, pay with QRIS or mix in your PawCoin, regular delivery in 2–4 days. You'll also get a nudge when food is about to run out, plus picks tailored to your pet's age and weight — not ads.

ℹ️ The Health tab has moved: vet consultations now live on the Health Records page ("Start consultation").

🆕 **The feed looks different** — full-width photos, one clean column, easier on the eyes. Love a post? Just ❤️ it right from the home screen, no need to open it first.

✨ **Recommendations that actually get you** — the All tab now mixes tips, funny moments, and cat & dog posts in the right proportions. Scrolling never gets stale.

📸 **Post cards made for sharing** — turn any post into a gorgeous image card with its own QR code, and share it straight to Stories.

🐾 **A new-look pet profile page** — your pet's profile link now has a scrapbook style that's way prettier to send to friends. Visitors only see the timeline; health data stays private.

🪪 **Share your pet's ID card for free** — the watermarked version, one tap. Earn PawCoin every time you share 😉

✂️ **Tidier photo uploads** — photos that are too tall or too wide can be cropped right before you post.

🏷️ Featured posts and standout members now get special badges from the TailTopia team. Your pet's smaller milestones now show up in Notifications too.

Update now, shop for your pets, and show them off today!

快更新，给毛孩子买买买，顺便晒一晒！

## D. 素材备注（给市场 / 印尼同事）

1. **PawCoin 那句话有前提。** 代码里身份证分享奖励**默认值全是 0**（总开关、单次、每日上限、月上限），上线时若运营没在后台「分享奖励」配好并开启，用户分享是拿不到币的。**发版前二选一：** 运营先配好 → 文案保留；不配 → 三语里删掉「Dapat PawCoin tiap kali share 😉 / Earn PawCoin every time you share 😉 / 每次分享还能拿 PawCoin 😉」以及短版对应半句。
2. **Toko 随版上线（2026-08-27 拍板），已写进主标题与第一条。** 三个文案细节要核：① 配送时效「Reguler · 2–4 hari」取自代码文案，若上线时物流方案有变要同步；② 「补货提醒」「按年龄体重推荐」两项在代码里有（`recoTriggerConclusion` / `recoPersonalSubtitle`），但依赖运营配置商品数据，商品少时可能不触发，市场侧别在素材里承诺；③ **商品评价提交页（7-2）与退货 5-9 尚挡在 UX 稿**，文案里刻意没提「评价」「退货」——若上架前补齐再加。Toko 上线还需商品库有货、服务范围（雅加达？）配置到位，文案没写覆盖城市，请确认是否要加「saat ini melayani Jabodetabek」一类限定。
2a. **Health Tab 被 Toko 替换**是老用户最容易迷路的点，三语都用一行 ℹ️ 提示了问诊入口新位置，建议不要删。
3. **刻意不写的功能**（PRD 已有、用户无感或不宜宣传）：手机号采集与第 3 天软引导（属于运营触达，不当卖点）；推送权限引导条（FR-85）；顶置 / 推广卡（运营位，不向用户宣传）；首页推荐的具体配比数字（保留在 PRD，文案只说「更懂你」）。
4. **不要提**「访客日历」——PRD 有、本版产品决定不做。
5. **截图 / 预览图建议：** 新版通栏 Feed（带 ❤️）、帖子图片卡预览页（Share to Story 按钮）、剪贴簿风格 H5 宠物主页、身份证卡面页的「Bagikan」按钮。不要拍后台、不要拍 Toko。
6. **版本号：** 代码 `pubspec` 仍是 `1.1.6+11`，上架前研发需改为 **1.2.0**，商店后台填的版本号与文案标题一致。
