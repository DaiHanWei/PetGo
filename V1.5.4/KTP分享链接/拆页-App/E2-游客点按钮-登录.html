E · App 内访客卡页（新页面）E · In-app visitor card page (new page)

装了 App 的人点链接直接进 App（FR-263）。卡主人进自己的卡详情（B1 / B2），其他人进访客卡页。游客也能看，点按钮才要登录。路由不在 /profile 下（那里都要登录）。示例卡：B5 分享出去的那张，Oyen 的 KTP Semanggi（No. TT230326020110，链接 /k/8fK2xQ），E、F 两条泳道都是这张。

With the app installed, the link opens the app directly (FR-263). The owner lands on their own card detail (B1 / B2); everyone else on the visitor card page. Guests can view; the buttons ask them to log in. The route is outside /profile (everything there requires login). Sample card: the one shared in B5, Oyen's KTP Semanggi (No. TT230326020110, link /k/8fK2xQ); lanes E and F both show this card.
