### 2.2 English — 2007 / 4000

```
Pet-friendly places have arrived.

FIND PLACES THAT WELCOME PETS
Browse cafés, parks, restaurants, malls, hotels and pet services near you, sorted by distance. Each place shows photos, what exactly makes it pet-friendly — pets allowed inside, outdoor seating, a pet menu, an off-leash area, leash required, large dogs welcome — and how other owners rated it. Filter by type or tag to find the one you need.

MARK THE SPOTS YOU LOVE
Found somewhere great? Drop a pin on the map, add up to nine photos and tell everyone why it works for pets. You can also add photos to places other people marked, leave a comment, and give a thumbs up or down. Share any place with friends — the link opens even if they don't have the app yet.

MEET THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PETS
Tap any avatar to open a full profile: their pets, everything they've posted, and how many likes they've earned. Tap a pet to browse its diary.

KNOW YOUR PET
Your pet's ID card now lives in a new Know Your Pet section, alongside a pet age converter. See how old your cat or dog is in human years — dogs are calculated by size — and share the result as a card. Sharing earns PawCoin.

MENTION FRIENDS
Type @ in a post or a comment to mention someone you've interacted with.

DISCOVER OTHER PETS
A new collection of pets to browse, on the Social tab and on your Diary.

SHOPPING, IMPROVED
• Tick only the items you want and check out just those
• Variants now carry their own photos, and the best available one is selected for you
• Reorder reminders show the price
• Search and page through the full catalogue
• See your order summary before you add an address
• Clearer payment results — declined, expired and cancelled are no longer the same message

ALSO IN THIS UPDATE
Post pages have been redesigned: photos keep their original shape and run edge to edge, and likes, sharing and comments now share one bottom bar. Milestones you missed are celebrated the next time you open the list. Plus dozens of fixes across chat, places, shop and sharing.
```

### 2.3 中文 — 697 / 4000

```
宠物友好场所来了。

找到欢迎宠物的地方
浏览你附近的咖啡店、公园、餐厅、商场、酒店民宿和宠物服务，按距离排序。每个场所都有照片、到底哪里对宠物友好（允许入内、户外座位、宠物餐食、宠物活动区、需要牵引、大型犬友好），以及其他主人的推荐情况。按类型或标签筛选，快速找到你要的那一家。

标记你发现的好去处
发现了不错的地方？在地图上选点、最多传九张照片，告诉大家它为什么适合带宠物来。你也可以给别人标记的场所补充照片、留下评论、点推荐或不推荐。场所可以分享给朋友——即使对方还没装 App，链接也能打开。

认识宠物背后的人
点任意头像即可进入完整的用户主页：他家的宠物、发过的全部内容、收获了多少赞。点开宠物还能看它的成长日记。

认识你的宠物
宠物身份证搬进了全新的「认识你的宠物」入口，同时新增宠物年龄换算。看看你家猫狗相当于人类几岁——狗按体型分别计算——并把结果做成卡片分享出去。分享还能获得 PawCoin。

@ 提及好友
在帖子或评论里输入 @，就能提及和你互动过的人。

发现更多宠物
社区页和成长日记页新增宠物推荐集合，随时逛逛别人家的毛孩子。

商城更好用了
• 可以只勾选想买的商品单独结算
• 每个规格都有自己的图片，并会自动帮你选中最合适的那个
• 复购提醒卡片显示价格
• 完整商品目录支持搜索与翻页
• 还没填地址也能先看订单金额
• 支付结果更清楚——被拒、超时、取消不再是同一句提示

本次更新还包括
内容详情页改版：图片保持原始比例通栏展示，点赞、分享和评论合并到同一个底栏。错过的里程碑会在你下次打开列表时补上庆祝。另有聊天、场所、商城、分享等数十项修复。
```
